📣 ICSE 2027 Competition · Dublin, Ireland

The Clarification Challenge — clarify before you code

Can your coding assistant ask the right question? We are co-organizing the Clarification Challenge at ICSE 2027: build an assistant that spots underspecified requirements, asks the right clarifying question, and then writes code that matches what the user actually meant. The winning team gets free ICSE registration, and every ranked entry may submit a solution paper to the ICSE 2027 Competition Track.
