<section class="lab-section">
<h2 class="lab-section-title">About</h2>

The name <em>Jie</em> (杰) means <strong>greatness</strong> — so <strong>JWare</strong> means <strong>Great Software</strong>. We want to empower every student in the JWare Lab to build great software throughout their career.

JWare Lab (杰件实验室) investigates quality foundations and principles in AI-assisted software engineering — spanning the <strong>full software development lifecycle</strong> (design, coding, testing, review, deployment, and maintenance). We focus on quality and safety in the new era of AI-generated code, ensuring that AI tools enhance rather than erode engineering rigor at every stage.
</section>
